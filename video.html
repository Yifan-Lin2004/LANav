Real robot demonstration

LANav on a Unitree Go2

Open-vocabulary object goal navigation from egocentric RGB observations and language-specified goals.

Download videoRead the paper ↗

The policy receives an RGB image, an object goal, and the previous action. A lightweight adapter translates its discrete decisions into robot motion commands.

The real robot evaluation covers five categories — trash bin, box, plant, chair, and table — from two starting locations, with five repetitions per target–start pair. The video illustrates deployment behavior.
